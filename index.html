About Us

About Us

Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours Primary School is a private mixed school. It is a Christian founded school bracing children of every faith. Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours also has a Nursery wing that is fully equipped and giving excellent services. The school is located in Kamuli Municipality, Kamuli District along Brewer Road, Bunangwe zone. The school has been leading the district in PLE for the last ten years.

More About Us

More About Us

At Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours P/S, we are committed to providing bursaries, fostering professional development, and delivering impactful education that shapes bright futures for our pupils through the following aspects

Scholarships and Support

We offer bursaries to pupils with academic potential but lacking financial resources. Our program addresses financial challenges, ensuring every child can achieve their educational goals.

Professional Development

We invest in continuous professional development for our teachers. Regular training, workshops, and educational resources equip them with the latest teaching methodologies and best practices.

Our Impact

Since 2011, we have impacted Kamuli's education. Our pupils excel in higher education and professions. They contribute positively to communities.

Our Programs

Our Programs

At Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours P/S, we offer comprehensive education, focusing on core subjects, critical thinking, extracurricular activities, and technology integration to prepare students for future success.

Academic Excellence.

Our curriculum is designed to challenge and engage students, preparing them for higher education and beyond.

Extracurricular Activities

We offer a variety of extracurricular activities, including sports, arts, and music, to develop our students' talents and interests.

Student Support Services

We provide support services to help students with their academic and personal development, including counselling and mentoring.

Academic Programs

Our academic programs are designed to provide a balanced and comprehensive education. We offer:

  1. Early Childhood/Nursery Education: Laying a strong foundation in the early years through baby, middle and top class.

  2. Primary Education: Building on foundational skills with a focus on literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking.

  3. Extracurricular Activities: Offering diverse opportunities in sports, music, arts, and clubs to develop well-rounded pupils who will impact the world.

Religious Affiliation.

We nurture our pupils to fear God, as it is biblical that the fear of the lord is the beginning of wisdom (Proverbs 1:7).

Extracurricular Activities

We promote well-rounded development through sports like football and netball, creative expression in arts and music, and diverse clubs nurturing special interests and talents.

Special Programs

We provide tailored support with remedial classes, enrichment for gifted students, and counseling services to support emotional well-being and academic success.

Community Engagement and Service Learning

We foster community engagement through service projects, environmental education emphasizing sustainability and doing community outreaches.

Academics.

Health and Wellness

We promote health with education on nutrition and hygiene, regular physical activity for fitness, and nutritious meals supporting students' overall well-being.

What We Offer

What We Offer

01

Bursaries.

At Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours P/S, we believe in the potential of every child and offer bursaries to intellectually capable students lacking financial backing. Our bursaries program focuses on providing merit-based free education to deserving students, ensuring they have the opportunity to succeed.

02

Community Engagement

Located in Kamuli Municipality, Kamuli Mixed Happy Hours P/S is deeply rooted in the community, engaging with local families, particularly low-income farmers, to promote the importance of quality education. We engage with organizations to enhance our pupil's educational journey.

03

Why Choose Us?

We offer individualized learning tailored to each pupil's unique needs, delivered by experienced teachers committed to excellence. Our safe, nurturing environment allows pupils to focus on their studies, while our holistic approach ensures their overall development for all aspects of life.
